# VYVE Wellness - HBOT Messaging Brief

> PURPOSE: Agree the message for the Recovery Kickstart page and ads before any copy is written. Every section below is a decision to confirm, not a draft to edit.

Prepared: 28 August 2026. Page: go.vyvewellness.com/hyperbaric-oxygen-special. Offer: Recovery Kickstart, $147.

# The approach

**Lead with the mechanism, not the ailment.** The page opens by explaining what hyperbaric oxygen actually does inside the body, then earns the right to talk about benefits. One idea, explained plainly, before any promise is made.

**This is message matching, not a new idea.** The best performing ad in the account already does exactly this. Its opening line is "What does 30 minutes of pure oxygen do to your body," followed by pressure, concentrated oxygen, and only then the benefits. The landing page does not match it. It opens on conditions instead.

**Breadth stays, but it moves.** The audience remains wide. The difference is that the range of people we help is stated once, in a qualifying list, rather than spread across the page as competing promises.

# The mechanism

| Beat | What the page says |
|---|---|
| 1 | Your body runs on oxygen, and it carries that oxygen on red blood cells. Red blood cells are large, so they only reach tissue where blood is flowing freely. |
| 2 | Inside the chamber, pressure rises to about twice normal atmosphere. |
| 3 | Under that pressure, oxygen dissolves directly into the fluid part of your blood, not only into the cells that carry it. |
| 4 | That fluid reaches what red blood cells struggle to reach. Swollen tissue, tired muscle, areas where circulation has slowed. |
| 5 | Oxygen is the raw material your body repairs and makes energy with. Give it more, and you support work it is already trying to do. |
| 6 | It compounds. One session is what it feels like. A course is where it goes. |

**Beat 6 carries more weight than its length suggests.** It sets the expectation that this is the first step of a plan rather than a one-off experience, which is the single cheapest change available to the page.

# Headline options

| Option | Line | Note |
|---|---|---|
| A | Oxygen goes where blood cannot | Delivery framing. Already approved as the section headline in v2 and the clearest of the four. |
| B | Your body knows how to repair itself. It just runs out of oxygen. | Raw material framing. Warmest and least technical. |
| C | Pressure changes what your blood can carry | Physics framing. Accurate, but weaker standing on its own. |
| D | It's not age. It's oxygen. | Deficit framing. Strongest hook, but needs a mechanism subhead under it. Page only, Meta reads this as a personal health attribute. |

**Recommendation: D as the headline, A as the subhead beneath it.** D names the assumption the audience already holds, and A immediately supplies the reason. D does not run in Meta ads.

# Benefits, in order

| Rank | Benefit | Why it sits here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Faster recovery | Broadest and most credible. It is also the first benefit the winning ad names. |
| 2 | Less inflammation and swelling | The mechanism explains this one most directly, and it quietly underpins ranks 1, 3 and 4. |
| 3 | Mental clarity and focus | What both existing reviews actually describe. Highest emotional pull for the core audience. |
| 4 | Energy and stamina | The running on empty angle. Sells the deficit the mechanism resolves. |
| 5 | Tissue and wound repair | The most clinically defensible item on the list, but the least persuasive. Use it as credibility, not as a lead. |
| 6 | Sleep quality | Include only if the clinic sees it consistently. |

**There is a gap between what we promise and what we can prove.** The copy leads on recovery and inflammation, but the two reviews on the page both describe clarity and fatigue. Either the new testimonials cover ranks 1 and 2, or the benefit order flips to match the proof available. This is a decision for the clinic, based on which testimonials are easier to obtain.

# Why this beats what you are already taking

| Beat | The argument |
|---|---|
| 1 | You have already tried the invisible things. Capsules, powders, greens, sleep trackers, ice baths. You take them on faith because you cannot feel any of them working. |
| 2 | A supplement has to survive your gut before it reaches anything. Digest, absorb, circulate, and then hope enough of it arrives where you wanted it. |
| 3 | Oxygen is not a supplement. It is what your body already runs on, and pressure puts it straight into your blood. Nothing to digest, nothing to absorb. |
| 4 | And you feel the session while you are in it. |

**The guardrail on this section.** We compare to the category, never to a named brand or product, and we never say supplements do not work. The line is that they are indirect and this is direct. A physician-led clinic telling people to bin their supplements reads as a sales pitch. The same clinic explaining why direct beats indirect reads as expertise, which is what the page is actually selling.

# Who it is for

Stated once, as a qualifying list:

- Athletes and active people who train hard
- Anyone recovering from surgery, injury or illness
- Sports injuries and slow healing tissue
- Brain fog and difficulty focusing
- Persistent aches and inflammation
- Running on empty despite sleep, diet and supplements

**A short "who this is not for" line makes the whole list more believable.** Hyperbaric oxygen has real contraindications and the clinic already screens for them. Putting a plain version of that on the page costs nothing and buys credibility that no amount of copy can.

# Physician-led

**This is the strongest competitive position available and the page currently buries it.** Anyone in Charlotte can sell chamber time out of a recovery bar. Four things only VYVE has:

- Dr. Haas on site. Board certified, physician led, not a wellness counter.
- The consultation is included in the $147, not sold as an upsell. The patient is not buying chamber time, they are buying a clinician's read on why they feel the way they do.
- The patient leaves with a plan on paper. An HRV baseline and a personalised hyperbaric plan. A session rental business cannot produce this.
- The patient is screened before going in. Safety framed as a benefit, not as a disclaimer.

**The line: anyone can sell you a session. Nobody else builds you a plan.**

# Reviews

Two are needed, with signed releases. What makes a review usable on this page:

- **Experience, never diagnosis.** "I had more energy by week two" works. A named condition being resolved cannot go on the page.
- **Split them across the benefit list.** One covering recovery or aches, one covering clarity or energy. Both current reviews sit in the clarity half, which leaves the top of the benefit order unproven.
- **First name, photo or initials, and what brought them in.** The reason for coming persuades more than the outcome, because it tells the reader that this is them.
- **The existing Lyme review cannot carry over** in any edited form.

**Existing Google reviews are the cheapest source.** If any already say the right thing, the clinic asks those patients for permission rather than sourcing testimonials from scratch.

# Compliance guardrails

Applies to every ad, page and email in this funnel.

| Rule |
|---|
| Never cure, treat, heal, reverse or fix. Always support, recovery, and how you feel. |
| "FDA-cleared" describes the chamber, never the benefit. It never attaches to energy, focus or recovery. |
| "Over 10,000 clinical studies" needs a source or gets removed. |
| No diagnostic framing in Meta ads. "Tired of running on empty" is out. Aspirational framing is in. |
| Every page carries: Individual results vary. This is a wellness service and is not a substitute for medical care. |
